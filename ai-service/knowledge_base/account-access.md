# Account Access

Customers may contact support when they cannot log in, have forgotten their password, or are unable to access their account.

## Password Reset

If a customer has forgotten their password:
1. Ask the customer to use the password reset option on the login page.
2. The password reset link is sent to the customer's registered email address.
3. The reset link should be used within the validity period specified in the email.

## Login Problems

If a customer cannot log in:
1. Verify that they are using the correct registered email address.
2. Ask them to reset their password if necessary.
3. Ask them to retry after clearing browser cache or using another browser.
4. Check whether there is a known authentication service outage.

## Escalation

Escalate the issue to the account support team if the customer still cannot access the account after completing the standard troubleshooting steps.
