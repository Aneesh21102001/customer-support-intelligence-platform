# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund when they were charged for an order that was cancelled or when a duplicate charge occurred.

## Duplicate Charges

If a customer was charged twice for the same order:
1. Verify that both charges belong to the same order.
2. Confirm that the duplicate charge was successfully captured.
3. Create a refund request for the duplicate charge.
4. Inform the customer that the refund may take several business days to appear.

## Refund Status

If a customer has not received an expected refund:
1. Verify that the refund was successfully initiated.
2. Check the refund transaction status.
3. Confirm the payment method used for the original transaction.
4. If the refund was processed successfully, provide the customer with the expected processing timeframe.

## Escalation

Escalate the issue to the payments team if the refund remains unresolved beyond the expected processing timeframe or if the refund transaction shows an error.
